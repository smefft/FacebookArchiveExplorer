INTRODUCTION

This tool merely gives you a user friendly way to peruse your downloaded facebook data.
This way you don't have to dig through directories of json files to find the meaningful
stuff you may want to save even after you delete your account.

This application never has access to the internet or any of your files other than 
the downloaded facebook data folder that you give it access to. 

It is so far very much a beta version and I'm working on adding more features for 
the other parts of the download.


MACOS INSTALL INFORMATION

I do not pay the $99 a year to be an official Apple Developer, so your Mac
will say that the app is from an unknown developer and it will not open it on first try.
Do not click "Move to Trash", just close the Dialog.

To open it anyways afterward, go to Settings > Privacy & Security,
then scroll down to where it shows you what apps it stopped you from opening and click "Open Anyway".
Then open the app again and proceed as normal
